<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Swiss Style (International Typographic Style)
  
  **Core Principles**:
  - Objective photography and text
  - Asymmetric organization
  - Use of a grid system
  - Sans-serif typography as a primary design element
  
  **Color Philosophy**:
  - High contrast black and white base to emphasize readability
  - Bold "Accessibility Red" (#E63946) accent for the strike-through and calls to action
  - Subtle cool grays for secondary elements
  - Intent: Communicate clarity, precision, and the "restoration" of order/accessibility
  
  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - Split-screen hero: Large typography on left, visual demonstration on right
  - Modular content blocks aligned to a strict 12-column grid
  - Generous negative space to improve focus and readability
  
  **Signature Elements**:
  - Large, crossed-out "Re" in Times New Roman vs clean "Calibri"
  - Interactive type tester (toggle between fonts)
  - Bold horizontal rules/dividers
  
  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - Hover states that "clarify" or "sharpen" content
  - Smooth scrolling with snap points for key sections
  - "Before/After" slider for font comparison
  
  **Animation**:
  - Text reveals using masking (sliding up)
  - Staggered entrance for grid items
  - Subtle parallax on scroll
  
  **Typography System**:
  - Headings: Inter (Tight tracking, bold weights)
  - Body: Inter or Open Sans (High legibility)
  - Contrast elements: Times New Roman (for the "problem" state)
</idea>
</text>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Neo-Brutalism / Accessible Modernism
  
  **Core Principles**:
  - Raw, honest materials (digital equivalents)
  - High visibility and contrast
  - Unapologetic functionality
  - "Form follows function" taken to the extreme
  
  **Color Philosophy**:
  - Off-white/Cream background (#F8F9FA) to reduce eye strain
  - Deep Navy (#1D3557) for text instead of pure black
  - Vibrant Yellow (#FFD60A) for highlights/markers
  - Intent: Maximum accessibility while feeling contemporary and robust
  
  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - Card-based layout with thick borders
  - Overlapping elements with hard shadows
  - Sticky sidebar navigation
  
  **Signature Elements**:
  - "Sticker" style badges for features
  - Visible grid lines
  - Monospace accents for technical details
  
  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - Tactile buttons with deep press states
  - Marquee scrolling for "The News" section
  - High-contrast focus states
  
  **Animation**:
  - Snappy, instant transitions (no ease-in-out)
  - Elements "slam" into place
  - Hover effects that shift position (X/Y translation)
  
  **Typography System**:
  - Headings: Space Grotesk or Archivo Black
  - Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible (if available) or Verdana
  - Code/Tech: JetBrains Mono
</idea>
